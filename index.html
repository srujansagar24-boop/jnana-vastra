Engineering Woven Heritage Manuscripts

Welcome to the official digital repository and authenticity portal of Sri Krishnarajendra Silver Jubilee Technological Institute (SKSJTI). This portal accompanies the physical book format engineered using advanced Electronic Jacquard shedding technology.

Aim, Vision & Mission

Aim: To pioneer textile typography by converting ancient scriptural documentation into permanent structural jacquard woven books.
Vision: To transition traditional paper scripts into indestructible high-definition textile archives that endure generations.
Mission: To successfully execute precise CAD step-bindings, pick-and-pick weft insertion, and canvas-reinforced binding structures.

Technical Architecture

  • Institution: Govt. SKSJTI, Department of Textile Technology
  • Loom Mechanism: 5000-Hook Electronic Jacquard
  • Weave Architecture: Pick-and-Pick Double Weft
  • Substrate Stability: Canvas-Reinforced Book Page
  • Warp Density: 112 Ends Per Inch (EPI)
  • Weft Density: 56 Picks Per Inch (PPI)
